Our Process

From airspace to insight — in weeks, not years

Traditional ground-based forest inventories take months and disturb ecosystems. Sylviculture's end-to-end workflow delivers certified, reproducible results on a fraction of the timeline.

Mission Planning

We design flight paths for 100 % area coverage, accounting for terrain, regulatory airspace, and desired resolution.

Drone Deployment & Data Capture

Autonomous drones log LiDAR returns, RGB and multispectral images, and GPS ground control — all in a single pass.

Processing & 3D Reconstruction

Point clouds are classified, filtered, and merged into canopy height models, Digital Elevation Models, and species layers.

Expert Modeling & Interpretation

Our life-science specialists apply validated models to the processed data, generating projections, indicators, and recommendations.

Delivery & Ongoing Monitoring

You receive an interactive dashboard, GIS layers, and a narrative report. Schedule repeat surveys for longitudinal monitoring.

📡 LiDAR Technology

Light Detection And Ranging emits millions of laser pulses per second, recording precise 3-D coordinates even through dense canopy. The result: a full-waveform point cloud accurate to ±2 cm — far beyond what photogrammetry alone can achieve.

🌿 Life-Science Expertise

Our modeling team holds PhDs in forest ecology, plant physiology, and spatial statistics. Every model is calibrated with field-validation plots and peer-reviewed literature, giving you audit-proof outputs for carbon registries and regulatory submissions.

🔁 Longitudinal Monitoring

Forests change. We set up repeat survey schedules — annually, seasonally, or after disturbance events — so you always have a current, comparable baseline and can quantify change with statistical confidence.

Applications

Built for every stakeholder

Whether you manage 50 hectares or 500,000, Sylviculture's outputs integrate into your existing workflows.

🌍 Carbon Credit Certification

Generate audit-ready carbon stock estimates aligned with VERRA VM0010, Gold Standard, and Plan Vivo methodologies. Reduce MRV costs by up to 70 % compared to traditional plot-based approaches.

🌲 Timber Volume & Yield Forecasting

Replace costly manual cruises with LiDAR-derived stand volume estimates accurate to within 5 %. Optimize harvest scheduling with multi-year growth projections.

🦠 Forest Health & Disease Surveillance

Detect early signs of bark beetle infestation, fungal pathogens, and drought stress via multispectral anomaly detection — months before symptoms are visible from the ground.

🔥 Fire Risk Assessment

Quantify canopy fuel loads, ladder fuel connectivity, and crown density to produce defensible wildfire risk maps for proactive treatment planning and insurer reporting.

🐦 Biodiversity & Habitat Assessment

Map structural complexity, vertical diversity, and rare species habitat across your property. Meet due-diligence requirements under the EU Biodiversity Strategy and TNFD framework.

🌱 Post-Harvest Regeneration Monitoring

Track replanting success, seedling height growth, and canopy closure over time. Validate FSC and PEFC certification commitments with independent, repeatable data.

📚 Academic & Conservation Research

Access raw point clouds, classified rasters, and model outputs under a data-sharing agreement. Ideal for long-term ecological research programs and national forest inventory contributions.

🏛️ Regulatory & Government Compliance

Supply national forest inventories, environmental impact assessments, and protected-area management plans with standardised, reproducible remote-sensing data.
